Side quest · In beta

What if you could read a creator’s whole week in one scroll?

Content-Story

Follow a creator across every platform and read it all as one story, with links back to every post.

See the beta

Why it exists

Keeping up with one creator meant X for the take, YouTube for the long version and Reddit for the argument. By the time I had pieced it together, I had lost the thread. I wanted something that did the piecing for me.

Before (Six apps): X for the hot take; YouTube for the long version; Reddit for the argument; Three more apps for the rest. After (One story): Every post in one readable story; Each line linked to where it came from; A verdict for each platform.

What’s on screen

  • One creator, six platforms: Pick who to follow once. It watches X, YouTube, LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok and Reddit for you.
  • Every line has a source: Small badges link each line back to the original post, so you can check it yourself.
  • A verdict per platform: See how the same take landed in each place. Loved on one, argued with on another.

How it works

  • Follow: Pick a creator. Content-Story finds them across six platforms.
  • Stitch: Their posts and the comments around them are joined, in order, into one story.
  • Read: You get the story with a source on every line and a verdict for each platform.

Decisions, and why

  • Landing page before the product is done: Getting people in early beats polishing in private, so the beta page went up first.
  • Priced in rupees: The first users are brands, agencies and newsletter writers here, so the pricing is in rupees.
  • No final name yet: The name has to feel like a real object and carry an emotion. Content-Story holds the spot until one does.
