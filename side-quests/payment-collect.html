Side quest · Idea + POC

Most manufacturers still chase payments over the phone.

Payment Collect

An idea for manufacturers: pull overdue bills from Tally and send the reminders on WhatsApp, with a payment link.

Talk to me about it

Why it exists

Indian MSMEs wait 73 days on average to get an invoice paid (Recordent, 2026), and ₹7.34 lakh crore sits stuck in delayed payments (GAME, FISME and C2FO, 2024). In most factories I know, one person in accounts keeps Tally open and spends the day calling parties. I think most of that can run on its own.

Before (Today: phone and Tally): Someone checks Tally for who owes what; Calls each party, one by one; Ledgers sent by hand; The owner hears about it when cash gets tight. After (How I’d fix it): Overdue bills read straight from Tally; A WhatsApp reminder with the ledger and a payment link; Firmer reminders on a schedule, calls only when needed; Paid invoices mark themselves.

What’s on screen

  • Ledger and pay link in one message: The party would get their ledger PDF and a one-tap payment link on WhatsApp. So nobody has to reply asking for the details.
  • Firmer, on a schedule: Polite on the due date, firmer at day 7. VIP parties never get the firm one.
  • A person makes the hard call: At day 15 someone from accounts calls the party. At day 30 the owner gets an alert.
  • Paid marks itself: Money that comes in through the link would mark the invoice paid. Tally catches up when the receipt is entered.

How it works

  • Sync: A small connector would read outstanding bills from Tally while it’s open. Nobody re-enters anything.
  • Remind: On the due date the party gets a WhatsApp with their ledger and a payment link.
  • Escalate: Firmer at day 7, flagged for a call at day 15, owner alerted at day 30.
  • Settle: Paying through the link marks the invoice paid on the owner’s dashboard.

Decisions, and why

  • Tally stays: Manufacturers here run on Tally, and asking them to switch kills the deal. Payment Collect would read Tally and leave it as the source of truth.
  • WhatsApp over email: Industry numbers put WhatsApp open rates near 98% and email around 21%, so email was never really an option.
  • Meta’s API directly, no reseller: WhatsApp resellers add a markup on every message. Going direct would keep each reminder at a few paise.
  • A payment link in every message: If paying means hunting for bank details, people put it off. The link also tells us when they’ve paid, so nobody has to check the bank.
  • A person makes the day-15 call: A lot of these parties have bought from the same manufacturer for ten years. I wouldn’t want a bot calling them about money.
  • VIP parties skip the firm reminder: Some customers you just don’t send a stern message to. The owner tags them once and they never get one.

So far

  • 73 days average wait to get an MSME invoice paid (Recordent, 2026)
  • 22% lower DSO when receivables are automated (PYMNTS and Mastercard)
  • 98% WhatsApp open rate, vs about 21% for email (industry benchmarks)
  • Nov 2026: Research into how manufacturers chase payments
  • Nov 2026: A POC on a test Tally company to see how it should feel

It isn’t a product yet. I built a rough POC on a test Tally company to work out how the Tally sync, WhatsApp reminders, payment links and the owner’s view should fit together.
