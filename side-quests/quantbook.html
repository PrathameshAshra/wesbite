Side quest · Personal tool

Testing a trading idea should take one sentence.

Quantbook

A backtesting notebook for Indian stocks. Test an NSE strategy, hedge it with options, and see how it would have done.

Why it exists

Every trading idea I had for Indian markets meant writing another backtest by hand. Most of them never got tested because the setup took too long. I wanted to just type the idea and see what happens.

Before (Notebook): Write the backtest by hand; Wire up data for every idea; Most ideas never get tested. After (One sentence): Type the theory in plain English; Claude builds and runs the backtest; Charts to compare, then decide.

What’s on screen

  • The theory, in plain words: Type the idea the way you would say it out loud. No code.
  • Strategy vs hedge: The backtest draws the strategy with and without its options hedge, side by side.
  • Notes that carry over: A running notes stream, so each new study builds on the last one.
  • Paper only: Nothing is bought or sold here. If a strategy holds up, I place the trade myself.

How it works

  • State: Type a theory about Indian stocks or F&O in plain English.
  • Test: It pulls data from Zerodha Kite, builds the backtest and runs it.
  • Compare: Charts show how it would have done, hedged and unhedged. Then I decide.

Decisions, and why

  • Dropped the notebook: Writing cells was the slow part. Now I state the theory and the app does the rest.
  • Its own app instead of a ChatGPT plug-in: An installable app with its own notes stream keeps every study’s context in one place.
  • Kept light on data: Kite API calls add up, so it favours index-level strategies over huge stock lists.
