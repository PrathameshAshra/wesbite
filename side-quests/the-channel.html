The Channel

Long-form videos on building AI products, from someone who was an engineer before he became a PM.

Why it exists

Most of what I watch on AI products is made by engineers or by PMs, rarely by someone who’s done both. I have, so that’s the channel: how AI products actually get built, explained for PMs by someone who used to write the code.

Before (Two camps): Engineers talk models and metrics; PMs talk users and roadmaps; Nobody translates. After (Both sides): Engineering ideas, explained for PMs; Examples from real product work; Long enough to go deep.

What’s on screen

  • Long-form: Episodes run long enough to actually explain things, as video and as a podcast.
  • First up: evals: “Evals explained for PMs by an ex-engineer”, in the works now.
  • One idea per episode: Each episode takes one engineering idea PMs keep running into and makes it usable.

How it works

  • Pick one idea: One engineering idea that PMs keep running into.
  • Show it: Explain it with a real example.
  • Ship it: Out as a long-form video and a podcast episode.

Decisions, and why

  • Long videos over shorts: Long video and podcast is the format I can actually keep up every week.
  • No ads, no sponsors: I’m not doing this for money. I just want to share what I’m learning.
  • Scrapped the first example: The first example I built for the evals episode didn’t feel real enough, so I dropped it. Still looking for the right one.

  • Oct 2026: Channel planned, first topic picked
  • Now: Evals episode in the works
