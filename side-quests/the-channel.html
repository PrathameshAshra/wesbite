Side quest · In production

How AI products actually get built, from the person writing the code.

The Channel

Long-form videos on building AI products, from an engineer who builds them for a living.

Why it exists

Most of what I watch on AI products is either hype or a demo that breaks the moment real users touch it. I build LLM features that real people use at work, so that’s the channel: what it actually takes to ship them, explained for anyone building one.

Before (What’s out there): Hype threads and launch videos; Demos that skip the hard parts; Little from people shipping it. After (The channel): One build problem per episode; Examples from real product work; Long enough to go deep.

What’s on screen

  • Long-form: Episodes run long enough to actually explain things, as video and as a podcast.
  • First up: evals: “Evals, explained by an engineer”, in the works now.
  • One idea per episode: Each episode takes one problem you hit when you build with LLMs and works through it.

How it works

  • Pick one problem: One thing that gets hard when you build with LLMs.
  • Show it: Explain it with a real example.
  • Ship it: Out as a long-form video and a podcast episode.

Decisions, and why

  • Long videos over shorts: Long video and podcast is the format I can actually keep up every week.
  • No ads, no sponsors: I’m not doing this for money. I just want to share what I’m learning.
  • Scrapped the first example: The first example I built for the evals episode didn’t feel real enough, so I dropped it. Still looking for the right one.

  • Oct 2026: Channel planned, first topic picked
  • Now: Evals episode in the works
