Social app · India

Pearbae

A social app for India where every profile is checked with Aadhaar and you can call someone without giving out your number. Built in about a year, now on the App Store and Google Play with 5K+ downloads.

Role: Front-end lead, via The Data Duck · Timeline: 1 year build · just launched · Stack: React Native, Node.js, WebRTC / VoIP, Real-time sync

  • 5K+ downloads on Google Play
  • 4.4★ Play Store rating
  • 7 cities hosting Pearbae nights

The problem

On most social apps in India you can’t tell who’s real. Pearbae wanted every profile verified, and the app still had to feel fun to use.

What I built

  • Aadhaar-based identity verification built into onboarding
  • In-app VoIP calling so numbers never have to be shared
  • Real-time chat, presence and live updates across the app
  • The app build through App Store review to launch on iOS and Android

The outcome

It took a year. Pearbae is live on iOS and Android, with 5K+ downloads on Google Play, a 4.4★ rating, and Pearbae nights running in Delhi, Pune, Chennai, Bengaluru, Mumbai, Hyderabad and Goa.
