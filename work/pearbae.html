Social app · India

Pearbae

A social app for India where every profile is checked with Aadhaar and you can call someone without giving out your number. Built and shipped to the App Store in about a year.

Role: Lead developer · Timeline: 1 year build · just launched · Stack: React Native, Node.js, WebRTC / VoIP, Real-time sync

  • 100% Aadhaar-checked
  • 1 yr idea to App Store
  • 0 phone numbers shared to call

The problem

On most social apps in India you can’t tell who’s real. Pearbae wanted every profile verified, and the app still had to feel fun to use.

What I built

  • Aadhaar-based identity verification built into onboarding
  • In-app VoIP calling so numbers never have to be shared
  • Real-time chat, presence and live updates across the app
  • The full iOS build through App Store review to launch

The outcome

It took a year. Pearbae is live in India now.
