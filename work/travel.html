Digital transformation · Travel

J M Mehta Tours and Travels

A travel agency that ran offline for 50 years. Over four years I moved the whole business online while it kept running.

Role: Tech lead · Timeline: 4 years · Stack: React, Node.js, Cloud

  • 50 yrs offline before we started
  • 100% online now
  • 4 yrs steady rollout

The problem

For 50 years the agency ran on phone calls, paper files and people’s memory. Growing meant moving online, slowly enough that nothing broke for customers.

What I built

  • One online flow, from the first enquiry to the booked trip
  • Internal tools that replaced the paper files
  • A slow rollout over four years, so the business kept running the whole time

The outcome

The agency runs fully online now.
